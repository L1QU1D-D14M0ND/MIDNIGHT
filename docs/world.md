## 2. SURVEILLANCE PROFILE (VISUALS)

*Directives for art that appears in flashbacks or recovered data files.*

- **SUBJECT APPEARANCE:** A tall, wiry woman in outdated, torn tactical gear. Her face is almost never clearly defined—usually obscured by rain, shadows, or static. In the few clear frames, she has pale skin and eyes that look completely black (dilated pupils). She does not look angry; she looks empty.
- **LOCATION:** Always depicted in the ruins of **"Sector Zero"** (The site of Operation: Downfall). It is always raining in her files.
- **KEY ITEM:** A curved, serrated **Trench Knife** (The "Reaper's Scythe"). It is rusted and stained. Scharnhorst is sometimes seen cleaning a similar knife in his own art.

## 4. INTELLIGENCE REPORT (LORE)

**BACKGROUND SUMMARY**

*"Operation: Downfall was not a battle; it was a containment breach. Command realized Elisabeth ('The Reaper') had become a natural disaster they could no longer control. They assembled 'MAJESTIC-12'—a team of twelve experimental prototypes and elite assets designed specifically to kill her. It was a trap. Elisabeth didn't just survive the ambush; she hunted them."*

**COMBAT LOG: THE MAJESTIC-12 INCIDENT**

- **TARGET 1 (LIQUID ZERO):** Tech expert. Killed first. Throat slit before the ambush began. The team was blinded instantly.
- **TARGET 2 (THE PROPHET):** Tactician. Outsmarted. Lured into his own minefield.
- **TARGET 3 (THE DARK):** Stealth Specialist. Bled out. Found with six precise shots to the chest in total darkness.
- **TARGET 4 (THE JUGGERNAUT):** Experimental MBT. Elisabeth breached the hatch and slaughtered the crew from the inside.
- **TARGET 5 (ROLLING THUNDER):** Elite Infantry Squadron. 40 confirmed KIA. No survivors.
- **TARGET 6 (THE PENTHOUSE):** Mobile Command. Burned to the ground with the commanders locked inside.
- **TARGET 7 (THE SILENCE):** Top-tier Hitman. Found with his neck snapped, facing the wrong way.
- **TARGET 8 (THE BLADELORD):** Melee Specialist. Decapitated. His own sword was broken; the wound matches the 'Reaper's Scythe'.
- **TARGET 9 (THE STALKER):** Marksman. Killed in CQC. His laser-guided missile launcher was stolen.
- **TARGET 10 (THE DREADNOUGHT):** Gunship. Shot down in one hit using Stalker's stolen weapon.
- **TARGET 11 (WHITE KNIGHT Mk. I):** The Prototype. Split in half. Elisabeth tore the experimental railgun from its chassis while the unit was still active.
- **TARGET 12 (BLACK KNIGHT):** Orbital Defense. Using the stolen railgun from White Knight, Elisabeth fired a desperate shot into the upper atmosphere, damaging the satellite and forcing it into dormancy. She succumbed to her wounds seconds after the shot.

**THE DEALER'S TAKE**

*"Twelve legends went into that valley to kill one woman. None of them walked out. The only thing that left that sector alive was a rookie Elisabeth took with her to her missions, shivering in a medevac chopper. That is why Scharnhorst doesn't sleep. He knows what 'The Best' looks like, and he knows he isn't even close."*

## 6. WORLD-BUILDING IMPACT

*Designer Notes: What aspect of the setting does this character represent?*

- **THEME:** **The Apex.** Elisabeth represents the absolute ceiling of power in this universe. She is the yardstick by which all other "Black File" units are measured—and found wanting.
- **NARRATIVE FUNCTION:** She creates a "Power Vacuum." The current era of warfare (Scharnhorst, White Knight Mk. II) is fighting in the shadow of the giants who died during Downfall.

### FACTION FILE: MAJESTIC-12

**CLASSIFIED ASSET FILE:** MAJESTIC-12 **CLEARANCE LEVEL:** COSMIC TOP SECRET // EYES ONLY **CODENAME ORIGIN:** The 12 "Majestic" Assets of the 1947 UFO Conspiracies / The Twelve Olympians **AFFILIATION:** Variable (The designation is shared; the loyalty is not) **TAGS:** [NONE] (The Cursed Platoon / Apex Predators)

#### I. VISUAL PROFILE (ARCHIVE FOOTAGE)

**Subject Description:** The file contains a single, heavily water-damaged photograph found in the wreckage of Sector Zero. It shows twelve distinct figures standing in front of a heavy transport plane. Their faces are crossed out with black marker, except for one young soldier in the back (Karras/Scharnhorst), whose face is circled in red question marks.

**The Symbol:** The faction crest is a **Hydra strangling a Globe**. In the modern era, this crest is rarely seen on uniforms. Instead, it is usually found stamped onto the chassis of destroyed experimental weapons or etched into the logic boards of rogue AI.

**Dynamic Evolution (The Legacy):**

- **The Golden Age (The Old Team):** The archive photos show uniformed heroes—shining armor, clean flags, united purpose. They look like a superhero team.
- **The Downfall (Sector Zero):** The footage is shaky, grainy, and horrifying. It shows "The Indestructible" Juggernaut burning. It shows "The Invisible" Dark engaging in panic fire. It shows gods dying like men.
- **The Modern Era (The New Team):** There is no group photo. The "New" Majestic-12 are never seen in the same room. They are depicted as shadows fighting shadows—a sniper on a roof aiming at a diplomat in a limo, both bearing the same Hydra tattoo.

#### II. PSYCHOLOGICAL PROFILE (LORE)

**The Philosophy:** "To be Majestic is to be a ghost. The original twelve were the greatest assembly of military power in human history. They were built to kill a god (Elisabeth), and they failed. The new twelve are not a team. They are a list of ingredients for a spell that went wrong. They do not trust each other. They do not like each other. They only share one thing: they are the only ones on the board who know how the world really ends."

**The Dealer's Note:** "You want to build a deck with them? Good luck. They aren't 'Super Friends. The Old Team died because they stood together in a kill zone. The New Team survives because they stay away from each other somewhat. If you see two Majestic-12 units on the same battlefield, it's not a reunion."

#### III. HISTORICAL RECORD: THE ORIGINAL COHORT (KIA)

**The Casualty Report (Operation: Downfall)** *Command has unsealed the identity of the original assets to serve as a warning to the new generation.*

1. **LIQUID ZERO (The Technopath):** *Status: KIA.* Throat slit before combat initiation. The network went dark instantly.
2. **THE PROPHET (The Tactician):** *Status: KIA.* Lured into his own minefield. The enemy knew his plan better than he did.
3. **THE DARK (The Ghost):** *Status: KIA.* Found with six precise shots to the chest in a zero-visibility zone.
4. **THE JUGGERNAUT (The Fortress):** *Status: KIA.* Experimental MBT. The enemy breached the hatch and neutralized the crew from the inside.
5. **ROLLING THUNDER (The Legion):** *Status: KIA.* Elite Infantry Squadron. 40 confirmed casualties. No survivors.
6. **THE PENTHOUSE (The Voice):** *Status: KIA.* Mobile Command Center. Burned to the ground with the High Command locked inside.
7. **THE SILENCE (The Assassin):** *Status: KIA.* Neck snapped. Forensic analysis suggests he never saw the target.
8. **THE BLADELORD (The Duelist):** *Status: KIA.* Decapitated. His proprietary monofilament sword was shattered.
9. **THE STALKER (The Hunter):** *Status: KIA.* Killed in CQC. Weapon system stolen by the enemy.
10. **THE DREADNOUGHT (The Sky):** *Status: KIA.* Aerial Gunship. Shot down by a single ground-to-air impact using Stalker's stolen weapon.
11. **WHITE KNIGHT Mk. I (The Prototype):** *Status: Destroyed.* Physically torn in half. Railgun assembly ripped from the chassis.
12. **BLACK KNIGHT (The Shield):** *Status: Dormant.* Orbital platform. Suffered catastrophic damage from a surface-to-orbit railgun shot. Systems offline for decades.

#### IV. CURRENT STATUS: THE NEW COHORT

**Operational Directives:** The "New" Majestic-12 designation has been reactivated, but the doctrine has been rewritten in blood.

- **The BLACK KNIGHT:** the only thing that survived the “Downfall” reactivated a few years ago and is the only original member from the old MAJESTIC-12
- **Deployment:** **NON-CONSOLIDATED.**
  - **The "Basket" Protocol:** Multiple assets may converge on a single Area of Operations (AO) if dictated by individual objectives, but they must *never* operate as a single tactical column. Command will never again risk the entire pantheon in one valley. If two Majestic-12 units are in the same sector, it is because their separate agendas have crossed paths, not because they are standing in formation.
- **Loyalty:** FRACTURED. Assets are currently embedded in opposing factions (Global Defense, The Clandestine Service, The Private Sector).
- **Objective:** SURVIVAL. The new cohort is not tasked with hunting Elisabeth. They are tasked with preventing the next Downfall.

**Placeholder membership:** Black Knight is the only confirmed member of the new cohort. A Black File whose clearance is stamped MAJESTIC-12 temporarily joins the faction until the roster is revised. That stamp is a placeholder, not a permanent assignment and not a rules tag. The placeholders are White Knight, Scheherazade, Janus, and Enterprise. Where a file already names another department, that department stays beside the placeholder line.
