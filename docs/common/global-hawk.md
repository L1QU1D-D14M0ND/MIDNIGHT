# RQ-4 Global Hawk

> Common asset. Up to eight copies. Rules: [Mechanics](../mechanics.md). Index: [Common cards](README.md).

A high-altitude reconnaissance drone. It watches your own draw pile, not the enemy's. The Scout does that job.

The Global Hawk does not make ordinary attacks. It occupies its slot and blocks commander strikes through that lane.

## Stage effect: Own Pattern

Inverse Scaling. The drone is useful early and goes quiet late.

- **Early.** At **5 to Midnight**, **4 to Midnight**, and **3 to Midnight**, at the start of your turn, look at the top card of your draw pile.
- **Late.** At **2 to Midnight** and closer, that look does not happen.

Janus's False Narrative uses the other stage of this effect. Gaslight can restore the look for one turn by telling the Global Hawk to use the early stage.
