## Stage effect: Flight Hours

Standard Scaling. The gunship is expensive early and cheaper late. Pay the chip cost of the stage in effect when you deploy it.

- **Early.** At **5 to Midnight**, **4 to Midnight**, and **3 to Midnight**, the Chip cost is 4.
- **Late.** At **2 to Midnight** and closer, the Chip cost is 2.

Janus's False Narrative uses the other stage of this effect. Total War charges 2 Chips while the real clock is still at **3 to Midnight**. Peacetime charges 4 Chips from **2 to Midnight** on.
