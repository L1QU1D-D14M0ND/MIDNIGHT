# Attack Chopper

> Common asset. Up to three copies. Rules: [Mechanics](../mechanics.md). Index: [Common cards](README.md).

A single-rotor gunship. It uses a lane slot. It is not an Orbital.

## Stage effect: Flight Hours

Standard Scaling. The gunship is expensive early and cheaper late. Pay the chip cost of the stage in effect when you deploy it.

- **Early.** At **5 to Midnight**, **4 to Midnight**, and **3 to Midnight**, the Chip cost is 4.
- **Late.** At **2 to Midnight** and closer, the Chip cost is 2.

Janus's False Narrative uses the other stage of this effect. Total War charges the late cost, 2 Chips, while the real clock is at **5 to Midnight**, **4 to Midnight**, or **3 to Midnight**. The stamp is **3 to Midnight**, so at **5 to Midnight** the chopper still cannot be deployed, and at **4 to Midnight** it can only enter by Breach. Breach pays twice the Chip cost in effect, then the clock advances, then the card enters. Peacetime charges the early cost, 4 Chips, from **2 to Midnight** on.
