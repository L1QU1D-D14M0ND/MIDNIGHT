## Stage effect: Quiet Work

Inverse Scaling. The scout is stronger early and fades late.

- **Early.** At **5 to Midnight**, **4 to Midnight**, and **3 to Midnight**, at the start of your turn, look at the top card of the enemy draw pile.
- **Late.** At **2 to Midnight** and closer, that look does not happen.

Janus's False Narrative uses the other stage of this effect. Gaslight can restore the look for one turn by telling the Scout to use the early stage.
