## Stage effect: Seasoned

Standard Scaling. The squad is weaker early and stronger late.

- **Early.** At **5 to Midnight**, **4 to Midnight**, and **3 to Midnight**, Rifle Squad has no bonus.
- **Late.** At **2 to Midnight** and closer, Rifle Squad has +1 Attack.

Janus's False Narrative uses the other stage of this effect. Total War grants the +1 Attack while the real clock is still at **5 to Midnight**, **4 to Midnight**, or **3 to Midnight**. Peacetime removes it from **2 to Midnight** on.
