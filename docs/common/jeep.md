# Jeep

> Common asset. Up to three copies. Rules: [Mechanics](../mechanics.md). Index: [Common cards](README.md).

## Drive up

Once each turn, during your main phase, if the Jeep is in your back slot and your front slot in that lane is empty, you may move it forward.

This card has no stage effect.
