# Stealth Fighter

> Common asset. Up to eight copies. Rules: [Mechanics](../mechanics.md). Index: [Common cards](README.md).

A low-observable fighter. The prototype's F-35 Lightning II and Su-57 Felon are this card. The Eurofighter Typhoon is the fighter without this restriction.

## Low observable

Infantry, Armor, and Heavy units cannot target it. Air and Support units can.

This card has no stage effect.
