# M142 HIMARS

> Common asset. Up to eight copies. Rules: [Mechanics](../mechanics.md). Index: [Common cards](README.md).

A truck-mounted rocket battery. It is heavy artillery, stamped **2 to Midnight**. The Field Gun is the towed piece that shoots down its own lane.

## Shoot the next lane

When the HIMARS attacks, choose its own lane or an adjacent lane. The attack uses the normal lane order in the chosen lane.

This card has no stage effect.
