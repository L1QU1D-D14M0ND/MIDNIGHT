# Strategic Battery

> Common asset. Up to eight copies. Rules: [Mechanics](../mechanics.md). Index: [Common cards](README.md).

A fixed long-range air-defense site. Naval support and the heavy batteries share this card. The prototype names are Aegis Ashore, MIM-104 Patriot, and THAAD Battery.

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Chip cost | 4 |
| Stamp | 2 to Midnight |
| Health | 5 |
| Attack | 0 |
| Tags | Structure, Heavy |
| Type | Structure |

The Strategic Battery does not make ordinary attacks. It occupies its slot and blocks commander strikes through that lane.

## Engage the raid

Once each turn, during your main phase, deal 2 damage to an enemy Air unit in this lane or an adjacent lane. This does not damage a commander.

This card has no stage effect.
