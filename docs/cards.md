## 2. VISUAL PROFILE (CARD ART)

*Directives for the art team to establish the "Poker Table" aesthetic. Target Style: 90s Anime / Gritty OVA (High contrast, mature tone).*

- **SUBJECT:** White Knight is a pristine, remotely controlled orbital spaceplane (similar to the X-37B but significantly more predatory). Its hull is covered in hexagonal white ceramic heat-shielding that looks like dragon scales. It has forward-swept wings and a single, glowing blue ion engine.
- **GEAR/WEAPON:** It does not carry missiles. The nose cone opens to reveal a Coilgun (Magnetic Accelerator) that fires solid tungsten needles. It looks surgical, fast, and impossibly clean compared to the gritty aesthetic of ground units.
- **SETTING:** Low Earth Orbit (LEO), looking down at the curve of the Earth.

### DYNAMIC EVOLUTION (Visual Progression)

*How the card art changes as the Doomsday Clock ticks down.*

- **5 TO MIDNIGHT (Cold War):** The spaceplane is sitting on a fog-covered runway at night, bathed in floodlights. Liquid nitrogen is venting from the fuel lines. It looks dormant but tense.
- **3 TO MIDNIGHT (Conflict):** Ascending. The background is the sky turning from blue to black. The thrusters are burning white-hot as it punches through the atmosphere.
- **MIDNIGHT (Doomsday):** Stabilized in orbit. The bay doors are open, and the Coilgun is extended, glowing with magnetic charge. It hangs perfectly still against the stars, a white cross over the burning world below. It does *not* dive; it simply aims.

## 7. DESIGNER'S NOTES (THE TABLE FEEL)

- **PHYSICALITY:** White Knight is the "Boogeyman" for high-level decks. Its presence forces the opponent to play honestly. If they try to hide behind Richelieu's politics or Scharnhorst's Protocol, White Knight simply ignores it. Visually, the card feels fast and sharp—a stark contrast to the heavy, messy aesthetic of the other units.
- **PSYCHOLOGY:** By hovering in orbit (Deployment Zone), it creates a sense of imminent doom. The opponent knows they cannot hide their key units. It forces a "Kill or Be Killed" mentality, as they must find Anti-Air solutions immediately or lose their best cards to the "Rod from God."

### MECHANICAL CHECK:

- **Does this respect the Decapitation Strike rule?** YES. The "Rod from God" targets units, not the Commander directly.
- **Does the Midnight effect follow priority?** YES. Atmospheric Breach happens on entry, clearing the way for subsequent actions.

- **GEAR/WEAPON:** It has no visible guns. Instead, panels on its surface are shifting like puzzle box pieces, revealing dull, copper-colored lenses and kinetic rod bays… and a hole punctured across the chassis.

### DYNAMIC EVOLUTION (Visual Progression)

*How the card art changes as the Doomsday Clock ticks down.*

- **5 TO MIDNIGHT (Cold War):** A grainy, black-and-white photograph taken from a U-2 spy plane. The object is a silhouette against the stars. It looks dormant.
- **3 TO MIDNIGHT (Conflict):** The object is backlit by the sun. Thrusters are firing (visualized as cold gas plumes). The lenses are glowing angry red. It has rotated to face the surface.
- **MIDNIGHT (Doomsday):** The satellite has fully unfolded. It does **not** crash. It hangs directly overhead, casting a supernatural shadow over the entire poker table. The "Kinetic Rod" bays are open, glowing with re-entry heat, looking like the Sword of Damocles waiting to drop.

"It doesn't answer our codes anymore. It was built to shoot down ICBMs in a war that never happened and was wasted to kill one person. Now, it just floats there, waiting for a heat signature that matches the Reaper’s. It is the shield that forgot it was supposed to protect us."

### THE DEALER'S NOTE

"The Black Knight isn't on your side. It's on the side of 'Silence.' It shoots down anything that flies too fast or explodes too loud. If you play this card, you are putting a lid on the war. Nothing goes up, nothing comes down."

## I. VISUAL PROFILE (CARD ART)

Subject Description:

Shadow Company is not a single hero. The art depicts a squad of "Faceless" operators. They wear high-end, unbranded tactical gear (tan and grey), quad-nods (night vision), and rolled sleeves. They look cleaner and better equipped than regular infantry, but they lack any flag patches.

The "Weapon" / Gear:

They are carrying modular carbines and tablet computers. In the background, there are stacks of Pelican cases and a private helicopter with no tail number.

**Dynamic Card Evolution (Visual Progression):**

- **5 to Midnight:** Men in expensive, dark grey business suits sitting in a boardroom, but they are wearing heavy tactical vests *over* the suit jackets. Balaclavas on.
- **3 to Midnight (Conflict):** They are in the field, moving efficiently through a ruin. They are checking their watches. The leader is arguing on a sat-phone.
- **Midnight (Doomsday):** The squad is boarding their helicopter, leaving the battle. One operator is looking back, shrugging. The card frame is empty except for the dust from their takeoff.

## II. PSYCHOLOGICAL PROFILE (LORE)

**The Philosophy:**

*"Patriotism is a poor currency. Shadow Company accepts wire transfers. They are the 'Fixers.' They handle the logistics, the security, and the setup so the real army can do the dying. They are the most efficient soldiers on the board—right up until the moment the shelling starts. Then, they remember they aren't paid enough to die."*

**The Dealer's Note:**

*"Use them to fix your hand. Use them to set up your plays. But do not rely on them to hold the line at the end. When the world ends, contracts are void."*

## III. OPERATIONAL DIRECTIVES (GAMEPLAY)

**Archetype:** Deck Smoothing / Ramp / Hand Fixer

**Tag Interaction:** As a **Black File** unit, Shadow Company acts as **[INFANTRY]** but with **"Mercenary"** status.

- **Interaction:** They cannot be inspired by **Commanders** or **Siren** (Morale does not work on them). They are immune to **"Fear"** effects but susceptible to **"Bribes"** (like Richelieu's).

Core Passive: "Logistics Network"

Designer Note: This is pure QoL. It smooths out the RNG of card games.

Effect: As long as the Doomsday Clock is at **5 to Midnight**, **4 to Midnight**, **3 to Midnight**, or **2 to Midnight**, your Hand Size is increased by +2.

- **"Scouting":** At the start of your turn, look at the top card of your Deck. You may keep it there or move it to the bottom. (Scry 1).

Rules of Engagement (The Contract)

Designer Note: They are amazing early game, but become a liability as the game gets dangerous.

- **5 TO MIDNIGHT: "Consulting Fee"**
  - **Status:** *Retainer*
  - **Active Ability:** **"The Fix."** Shuffle 1 card from your Hand back into your Deck. Draw 1 new card. (Fix a bad hand).
  - **Flavor:** *"Optimizing assets. Removing redundancy."*
- **4 TO MIDNIGHT: "Outsourcing"**
  - **Status:** *Efficient*
  - **Upgrade:** The first unit you play each turn costs **-1 Chip**. (Ramp).
  - **Flavor:** *"We brought our own transport. No charge."*
- **3 TO MIDNIGHT: "Direct Action"**
  - **Status:** *Combat*
  - **Upgrade:** Shadow Company gains **+2 Attack**. They are now combat-capable.
  - **Flavor:** *"Target confirmed. We are going loud."*
- **2 TO MIDNIGHT: "Hazard Pay"**
  - **Status:** *Renegotiating*
  - **Penalty:** **"Logistics Network"** (Scry) is disabled. To attack or block with Shadow Company, you must pay **1 Chip**.
  - **Flavor:** *"This wasn't in the contract. We need a bonus."*
- **1 TO MIDNIGHT: "Breach of Contract"**
  - **Status:** *Retreating*
  - **Penalty:** Shadow Company cannot Attack or Block. They deal **1 Damage** to your Commander every turn (Theft).
  - **Flavor:** *"We're pulling out. Taking the equipment with us."*
- **MIDNIGHT: "GOLDEN PARACHUTE"**
  - **Status:** *Void*
  - **Final Resort:** **"Liquidation"**
    - **Effect:** Shadow Company returns to your hand. Draw **3 Cards**.
    - **Flavor:** *"Pleasure doing business. Good luck with the apocalypse."*

## I. VISUAL PROFILE (CARD ART)

Subject Description:

Phalanx is not a specialist team; it is a Battalion. The art depicts an endless wall of identical, heavily armored soldiers with riot shields and faceless visors. They are not hiding; they are standing in the open, shoulder-to-shoulder.

The "Weapon" / Gear:

They carry standard-issue assault rifles and heavy ballistic shields. There are no "cool" gadgets—just overwhelming uniformity and discipline. Flags are waving in the background.

**Dynamic Card Evolution (Visual Progression):**

- **5 to Midnight (Cold War):** "Peacekeepers." They are standing guard at a checkpoint, shields down, handing out rations to civilians. The lighting is harsh white sunlight.
- **3 to Midnight (Conflict):** "Riot Control." The shields are locked together. Tear gas canisters are flying. The line is advancing over rubble.
- **Midnight (Doomsday):** "The Meat Grinder." The formation is broken, uniforms are muddy and bloody, but they are fighting with entrenching tools and bayonets in a trench. There are thousands of them.

## II. PSYCHOLOGICAL PROFILE (LORE)

**The Philosophy:**

*"Mercenaries fight for a paycheck; they run when the money runs out. The Phalanx fights for the flag. They do not run. They do not ask for a raise. They dig a hole, they stand in it, and they die in it. They are the immovable object that the state is built upon."*

**The Dealer's Note:**

*"Shadow Company fixes your hand; Phalanx fixes your board. They hate each other. The Mercs call the Regulars 'Cannon Fodder.' The Regulars call the Mercs 'Cowards.' But when the line breaks, you don't want a consultant. You want a soldier."*

## III. OPERATIONAL DIRECTIVES (GAMEPLAY)

**Archetype:** Board Anchor / buffer / Token Swarm

**Tag Interaction:** As a **Black File** unit, Phalanx overrides **[MORALE]**.

- **Interaction:** Friendly units adjacent to Phalanx cannot be **"Feared"**, **"Bribed"**, or forced to **"Retreat"** (Return to Hand). They are locked into the combat zone.

Core Passive: "The Shield Wall"

Designer Note: This punishes the "Cut and Run" tactics of Shadow Company.

Effect: Friendly units gain +1 Armor for every adjacent friendly unit.

- **Anti-Mercenary:** If a friendly unit would be returned to your Hand (e.g., by Nightingale or Shadow Company's 'Golden Parachute'), it stays on the board and takes **1 Damage** instead. ("Deserters will be shot").

Rules of Engagement (The Mobilization)

Designer Note: Phalanx starts slow (Bureaucracy) but becomes an unstoppable tide as the nation mobilizes.

- **5 TO MIDNIGHT: "Rules of Engagement"**
  - **Status:** *Defensive*
  - **Ability/Passive:** **"Hold Fire."** Phalanx cannot Attack. Friendly units in its lane gain **"Guard"** (Intercept attacks).
  - **Flavor:** *"Do not fire until fired upon."*
- **4 TO MIDNIGHT: "Article 5"**
  - **Status:** *Authorized*
  - **Upgrade:** Phalanx can now Attack. When Phalanx attacks, summon a **"Conscript"** Token (1/1 Infantry) in the nearest empty slot.
  - **Flavor:** *"Reserves called up. Deployment authorized."*
- **3 TO MIDNIGHT: "Combined Arms"**
  - **Status:** *Advancing*
  - **Active Ability:** **"Fire Mission."** Deal damage equal to the number of Friendly Units on the board to a target enemy.
  - **Flavor:** *"Focus fire on grid square 4-Alpha."*
- **2 TO MIDNIGHT: "Mass Wave"**
  - **Status:** *Overwhelming*
  - **Upgrade:** "Conscript" Tokens now have **"Charge"** and **+1 Attack**.
  - **Flavor:** *"Send the next wave! And the next!"*
- **1 TO MIDNIGHT: "Scorched Earth"**
  - **Status:** *Fanatical*
  - **Passive:** Friendly units explode on death, dealing **1 Damage** to adjacent enemies.
  - **Flavor:** *"If we lose this ground, burn it."*
- **MIDNIGHT: "THE UNKNOWN SOLDIER"**
  - **Status:** *Martyrdom*
  - **Final Resort:** **"Last Stand"**
    - **Effect:** For this turn, your units **Cannot Die** (Health cannot drop below 1). At the end of the turn, Phalanx is destroyed.
    - **Flavor:** *"They shall not grow old, as we that are left grow old."*

## I. VISUAL PROFILE (CARD ART)

Subject Description:

Siren is not a soldier. She is a War Correspondent / Anchorwoman wearing a pristine press vest over a cocktail dress. She stands in front of a bank of monitors, holding a microphone like a scepter. She is beautiful, but her smile is slightly too wide, uncannily perfect.

The "Weapon" / Gear:

She is surrounded by camera drones and teleprompters. The background is not a battlefield, but a Broadcast Control Room (The Green Screen).

**Dynamic Card Evolution (Visual Progression):**

- **5 to Midnight (Cold War):** She is in a studio, perfectly lit, makeup flawless. The "News Ticker" at the bottom of the card frame reads: *PEACE TALKS SUCCESSFUL.* She is smiling warmly.
- **3 to Midnight (Conflict):** She is "on location" in a flak jacket, but the background is clearly a digital fabrication glitching at the edges. The makeup is running. The ticker reads: *CONTAINMENT BREACH.* She looks frantic.
- **Midnight (Doomsday):** The studio has collapsed. She is screaming into a dead microphone, covered in dust. The cameras are broken. The ticker reads: *NO SIGNAL.*

## II. PSYCHOLOGICAL PROFILE (LORE)

**The Philosophy:**

*"The Siren doesn't need to drag you onto the rocks; she just convinces you that the rocks are a safe harbor. She decides who the 'Terrorist' is and who the 'Freedom Fighter' is. She can turn a war crime into a tragic accident with a lower-third graphic. But you can only lie to the camera for so long before the explosion blows the lens out."*

**The Dealer's Note:**

*"She doesn't fire bullets. She fires headlines. Play her early, and she'll convince the enemy army to go home. But don't rely on her when the sky turns red. Nobody checks the news during the apocalypse."*

## III. OPERATIONAL DIRECTIVES (GAMEPLAY)

**Archetype:** Control / Pacifism / Anti-Aggro

**Tag Interaction:** As a **Black File** unit, Siren manipulates **[MORALE]**.

- **Interaction:** Enemy units attacking Siren suffer a **"PR Nightmare"** penalty. If they destroy her, the enemy Commander loses reputation (Chips penalty ticket) due to "Killing the Press."

Core Passive: "The Spin Room"

Designer Note: This forces the opponent to play honestly or suffer penalties.

Effect: As long as the Doomsday Clock is at **5 to Midnight**, **4 to Midnight**, or **3 to Midnight**, whenever an enemy unit Attacks, there is a 50% Chance the attack is "Censored" (Cancelled). The unit creates a "Breaking News" effect instead.

Rules of Engagement (The Narrative Collapse)

Designer Note: Siren is a god of manipulation early on. As the war escalates, the truth becomes undeniable, and her power fades.

- **5 TO MIDNIGHT: "Manufacturing Consent"**
  - **Status:** *Viral*
  - **Active Ability:** **"Character Assassin."** Target an enemy unit. It gains the **"Traitor"** tag. (Enemy units adjacent to it will attack IT instead of you next turn).
  - **Flavor:** *"Sources say he's working for the other side."*
- **4 TO MIDNIGHT: "Embargo"**
  - **Status:** *Controlling*
  - **Upgrade:** Enemy units with **[HEAVY]** or **[ARMOR]** cost **+2 Chips** to deploy.
  - **Flavor:** *"Public outcry over military spending halts deployment."*
- **3 TO MIDNIGHT: "Deep Fake"**
  - **Status:** *Manipulative*
  - **Active Ability:** **"Body Double."** Target a friendly unit. It creates a holographic copy of itself with 1 Health but full Attack.
  - **Flavor:** *"We have footage of them in two places at once."*
- **2 TO MIDNIGHT: "Credibility Gap"**
  - **Status:** *Glitching*
  - **Penalty:** "The Spin Room" chance drops to **0%**. Enemy units ignore PR penalties.
  - **Flavor:** *"They aren't listening to the broadcast anymore."*
- **1 TO MIDNIGHT: "Dead Air"**
  - **Status:** *Silenced*
  - **Penalty:** Siren cannot act. She is **Stunned** every turn.
  - **Flavor:** *"We've lost the feed! Cut the feed!"*
- **MIDNIGHT: "MASS HYSTERIA"**
  - **Status:** *Panic*
  - **Final Resort:** **"Broadcast Interrupt"**
    - **Effect:** Sacrifice Siren. Force a **"Ceasefire"** for 1 Turn. (No units can attack; no cards can be played). Move the Doomsday Clock back one minute. It cannot move earlier than **5 to Midnight**.
    - **Flavor:** *"Please stand by. This is not a test."*

## I. VISUAL PROFILE (CARD ART)

Scheherazade is not a soldier; she is a Crisis Engineer. She is depicted inside a deep-earth missile silo control room. She looks exhausted, wearing a rumpled shirt with sleeves rolled up, surrounded by red warning lights and screens flashing "LAUNCH IMMINENT."

The "Weapon" / Gear:

She holds no weapon. Her hands are gripping a massive manual override lever, knuckles white. She isn't turning the key; she is physically holding the mechanism back against the hydraulics.

## II. PSYCHOLOGICAL PROFILE (LORE)

**The Philosophy:**

*"There is no victory in the apocalypse. There is only silence. Scheherazade's job is to ensure the silence never comes. She unravels the launch codes. She cuts the fuel lines. She tells the computer a story that never ends. She buys us one more minute. And then another. And another."*

**The Dealer's Note:**

*"The enemy wants Midnight. They brought their Black Knight, their Enterprise. They are waiting for the bell. Scheherazade breaks the clock. As long as she is breathing, the nukes stay in the tubes, and the monsters stay in orbit."*

## III. OPERATIONAL DIRECTIVES (GAMEPLAY)

**Archetype:** Stall / Meta-Stabilizer / Legacy Support

**Tag Interaction:** As a **Black File** unit, Scheherazade overrides **[ORBITAL]** protocols.

- **Interaction:** Orbital units require Midnight to deploy. Because Scheherazade prevents the clock from entering **Midnight**, Orbital cards are "Brick" (useless) in the opponent's hand as long as she is on the board.

Core Passive: "The 1001st Night"

## I. VISUAL PROFILE (CARD ART)

Subject Description:

Cataphract is a human operator, but barely. They are encased in a massive, powered EOD-Class Exoskeleton that makes them look twice as wide as a normal soldier. The armor is pitted, scarred, and covered in reactive tiles. The helmet has no visor, only a solid steel faceplate with a cluster of cameras. This way no one can get inside and kill the pilot.

The "Weapon" / Gear:

The unit carries a Ballistic Shield the size of a door in one hand and a heavy auto-cannon underslung on the other arm. The suit vents steam and hydraulic fluid as it moves.

**Dynamic Card Evolution (Visual Progression):**

- **5 to Midnight (Cold War):** The suit is in a maintenance bay, stripped of its outer plating. Mechanics are welding the chassis. It looks industrial and ugly.
- **3 to Midnight (Conflict):** The suit is fully plated and walking down the center of a street. Small arms fire is sparking harmlessly off the chest. The operator is ignoring it.
- **Midnight (Doomsday):** The shield is shattered, revealing the operator's arm underneath, but they are still moving forward. The suit is glowing red from internal heat. They are physically pushing a destroyed tank out of the way.

## II. PSYCHOLOGICAL PROFILE (LORE)

**The Philosophy:**

*"Speed is a crutch for those who fear being hit. Cataphract does not run. It does not hide. It accepts the kinetic energy of the battlefield and swallows it. It is the physics of 'Mass over Velocity.' If you cannot go around it, and you cannot go through it, you lose."*

**The Dealer's Note:**

*"Put him in the front. Put your sniper behind him. Watch the enemy waste their entire turn trying to scratch the paint. Cataphract isn't flashy, but he is the reason your other units get to go home."*

## III. OPERATIONAL DIRECTIVES (GAMEPLAY)

**Archetype:** Tank / Protector / Mobile Fortress

**Tag Interaction:** As a **Black File** unit, Cataphract counts as both a **Unit** and a **[STRUCTURE]**.

- **Interaction:** It can be repaired by [SUPPORT] units like a building. It blocks Line of Sight. It takes double damage from "Bunker Buster" effects but takes 0 damage from Small Arms.

Core Passive: "Phalanx Doctrine"

Designer Note: This unit creates a safe zone.

Effect: Units directly behind Cataphract cannot be targeted by Ranged Attacks. Cataphract has "Taunt" (Must be attacked first) for units in its own lane.

Rules of Engagement (The Advance)

Designer Note: Cataphract gets slower but tougher as the war escalates, eventually becoming an immovable object.

- **5 TO MIDNIGHT: "Deployment"**
  - **Status:** *Heavy*
  - **Ability/Passive:** **"Hardened."** Cataphract ignores the first 1 damage from any source.
